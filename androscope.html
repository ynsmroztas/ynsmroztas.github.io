What it is

AndroScope is a rootless Android pentest framework. Patch an authorized lab APK once, load a Frida Gadget into that package, then run PREPARE → ASSESS → RUNTIME on a real unrooted phone.

It does not attach to a random Play Store process. The runtime lives inside the target you were allowed to instrument. Example package on this page is the fictional lab.sample.app.

Design rule: instrument the target. Do not pretend a second APK can police every other APK in the background.

How a live test feels

You walk the lab build. The transcript follows your hands. Empty log means you did not hit the surface.

What it is not

  • Not a Play Store product that silently hooks whatever you open.
  • Not a replacement for AndroHunter.
  • Not a universal Integrity / RASP / pinning kill-switch.
