The instrument

One app, mapped end to end.

AndroScope is not a background spy APK. It is an operator console plus a target-side runtime.

01 · PREPARE

Surface before you touch the UI

Manifest, components, schemas, DEX strings. Findings get FH-IDs.

02 · ASSESS

Radar, not a dump

Risk is calibrated — one exported editor is not a critical RCE.

You drive. The target is instrumented.
